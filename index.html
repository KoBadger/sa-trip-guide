🎒 Crucial Bag, Airport & Local Transit Hacks (Don't Skip!)

  • 📏 Frontier's Exact Free Bag Size: Your 1 free personal item must fit in the metal sizer at the gate: 14" H × 18" W × 8" D (including wheels/handles/straps). A normal soft backpack that isn't overstuffed fits easily. If it's too big at the gate, Frontier charges $60–$99!
  • 📱 Use the Free Frontier App to Check In: Frontier now charges up to $5 for website check-in and up to $25 if you ask an airport agent to print your pass. Download the mobile app and check in 24 hours before takeoff for $0.
  • 🧳 Southwest Bag Update (New in 2026): Southwest ended “2 free checked bags” on Basic fares — checked bags now cost $45 (1st bag) and $55 (2nd bag) each way. However, 1 full-size carry-on roller bag (10"×16"×24") + 1 personal item are still 100% FREE on Southwest!
  • 🚌 $1.30 Airport-to-Downtown Bus (Saves $30+ Uber): At San Antonio Airport, walk to the Lower Roadway (Arrivals/Baggage level) at the far west end of Terminal B and hop on VIA Bus Route 5. It goes straight to Downtown / River Walk in ~30 mins for just $1.30 (or buy a $2.75 unlimited Day Pass in the VIA goMobile+ app; seniors 62+, students & military pay half price: $0.65 ride / $1.35 day pass). Note: Express Route 7 is on construction detour, so take Route 5.

📖 Quick Plain-English Glossary

Round trip vs. One-way
Round trip = going there AND coming back on one ticket. One-way = just one direction.
1 stop (Layover)
You land in a middle city (Las Vegas or Denver) and switch to a second plane to finish the trip. Nonstop = one plane the whole way.
Basic Fare
The lowest-price ticket. Includes your seat and 1 small backpack/purse under the seat in front of you.
Pay in 4 vs. Monthly Financing
Pay in 4 splits the bill into 4 equal payments over 6 weeks with 0% interest (25% due today). Monthly financing spreads it over 3–18 months, which shrinks each payment but may add interest.
